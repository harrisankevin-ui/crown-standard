How It Works

The Pre-Wash Ritual.

Not a styling oil. Not a leave-in. A pre-wash treatment designed to work before the wash.

01

Apply

Apply the oil directly to your scalp and work it through your hair from root to tip. Focus on thinning areas and the entire scalp. Massage it in and let it absorb.

02

Leave

Let it sit for at least 20–30 minutes for results. For deeper conditioning, leave it on for an hour or more — or apply overnight and wash in the morning.

03

Wash

Shampoo out thoroughly. Use consistently — before every wash. The standard is built in the routine.

Use consistently. The standard is built over time.
