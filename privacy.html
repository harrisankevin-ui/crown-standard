Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Crown Standard ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you visit our website or make a purchase.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Name and email address when placing an order
  • Shipping address for delivery purposes
  • Payment information — processed securely by Stripe. We never store your card details.

Information Collected Automatically

  • Browser type and device information
  • Pages visited and time spent on our site
  • IP address and general location (country/city level)

How We Use Your Information

  • To process and fulfill your orders
  • To send order confirmation and shipping updates via email
  • To respond to customer service inquiries
  • To improve our website and product offerings
  • To send occasional marketing emails — only if you opt in

Payment Security

All transactions are processed through Stripe, a PCI-DSS Level 1 certified payment processor. Your card number, CVV, and banking details are never transmitted to or stored on our servers. Crown Standard only receives confirmation that a payment was completed.

Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your data only with:

  • Stripe — for payment processing
  • Shipping carriers — to deliver your order (name and address only)
  • Legal authorities — if required by law

Cookies

Our website uses minimal cookies to ensure basic functionality. We do not use tracking cookies for advertising. You can disable cookies in your browser settings without affecting your ability to browse the site.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Opt out of marketing communications at any time by emailing us

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hello@crownstandard.co

Data Retention

We retain order information for a minimum of 7 years as required by Canadian tax law. Marketing preferences and account data are retained until you request deletion.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of our website after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
