Questions

Everything You Need to Know.

Clear answers on how Crown Standard works, which formula to choose, and what to expect.

Which formula is right for me? +

Curl Standard is a richer blend for curly, coily, coarse, or dry hair — deeper nourishment and structural support. Straight Standard is lighter, for straight and wavy types — balanced moisture without weighing hair down.

What is the difference between Curl Standard and Straight Standard? +

Both share Pumpkin Seed, Coconut, Almond, and Flax Seed oils. Curl Standard adds Castor as the fifth oil for richer nourishment. Straight Standard adds Black Seed Oil as the fifth oil for lightweight scalp support.

Is this a leave-in oil or a styling product? +

Neither. Crown Standard is a pre-wash treatment applied to dry hair before washing. It is designed to support scalp health and the appearance of healthier, fuller hair. Apply before washing, let it sit at least 20–30 minutes, then shampoo out. It is not a styling or leave-in oil.

Can I use Curl Standard on straight hair (or vice versa)? +

Yes — both are safe for all hair types. The formulas are optimized for their intended textures, but both work regardless of hair type. Not sure? Start with Straight Standard for a lighter feel.

Is this designed for hair growth? +

Crown Standard is not a medical hair-loss treatment. It is formulated to support scalp health, create the conditions for healthier hair, and with consistent use, support the appearance of stronger, fuller growth. Results come from the routine — not a single application.

How often should I use it? +

Before every hair wash. Apply at least 30 minutes before shampooing. For deeper conditioning effects, leave it on for an hour or more. Consistency is where the standard is built.

Will it leave hair greasy? +

No. As a pre-wash treatment, it washes out completely during your regular cleanse. Shampoo thoroughly and your hair will be clean without any residue.

How should I store Crown Standard? +

Store in a cool, dark place away from direct sunlight. Cold-pressed oils are sensitive to oxidation — proper storage ensures your formula stays at its best.
